# AP English Literature — school text mapping (2026-27)

The 34-week plan keeps the nine College Board CED units in their existing order. School titles are attached to those units. No week-by-week classroom calendar was on the syllabus, so this file does not invent class dates beyond the plan's existing `startDate` / `dateRange` fields.

Classroom weighting from the syllabus: formative 20% (reading responses, annotations, discussion prep, practice-writes, some MCQs, writing process, presentations); summative 80% (final essays, projects, seminars, exams, research, some MCQs).

## Required texts

- *Literature & Composition*, Jago, Shea, Scanlon, and Aufses. Bedford/St. Martin's, 2011 — anthology for short fiction and poetry
- *Antigone*, Sophocles
- *Never Let Me Go*, Kazuo Ishiguro
- *Their Eyes Were Watching God*, Zora Neale Hurston
- Choice work of fiction (purchase or borrow) — independent Q3 literary-argument bank

## Sequence across the 34 weeks

| Weeks | CED unit | School text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Short Fiction I | Jago anthology stories (character, setting, narration, structure) |
| 4–5 | Poetry I | Jago anthology poetry (line/stanza, imagery, form) |
| 6–8 | Longer Fiction or Drama I | **Antigone** — first long work (Greek drama, structure, character, argument) |
| 9–10 | Short Fiction II | Jago anthology stories |
| 11–14 | Poetry II | Jago anthology poetry (figurative language, sound, comparison) |
| 15–17 | Longer Fiction or Drama II | **Their Eyes Were Watching God** (narration, dialect, setting, character) |
| 18 | Units 1–6 checkpoint | Q3 from memory on Antigone or Their Eyes Were Watching God |
| 19–21 | Short Fiction III | Jago anthology stories (unreliability, free indirect discourse) |
| 22–24 | Poetry III | Jago anthology poetry (paradox, irony, juxtaposition) |
| 25–28 | Longer Fiction or Drama III | **Never Let Me Go** plus ongoing **choice novel** for Q3 |
| 29–30 | Mixed review / error-log repair | Q3 bank: Antigone, Their Eyes Were Watching God, Never Let Me Go, choice novel |
| 31–34 | Exam review and exam week | Same Q3 bank; light Jago poem before the exam |

Antigone opens the year's long-work sequence (not the year itself). Short Fiction I still starts the plan so the existing CED progression is unchanged.

Practice set ids are unchanged (`lit-u1-set` through `lit-u9-set`). Passage sets remain original coaching texts; labels name the school work the week is studying.

Calculus BC, Statistics, and Physics 2 assignments were not remapped; no syllabi for those courses were supplied.
